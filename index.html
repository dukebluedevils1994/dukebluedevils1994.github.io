Executive Summary

I lead product strategy and execution for Bread Pay's lending products. That covers FlexLoan, a net-new multi-transaction loan product providing funds of up to $40K, and the merchant onboarding path that all merchants must take to onboard with Bread. I translate what enterprise merchants need into specs, and align engineering, compliance and business development so launches move faster. Over my time at Bread I've grown from associate PM to senior PM, shipping longer loan term products, a first-of-its-kind single rate APR loan offering for Bread, a compliance tool that cut regulatory incidents, and co-created a trade-in service product. This page is a snapshot of that scope and impact.
